Over a very short period, the pendulum rapidly loses its balance, falling significantly to the left from its initial stable position. Concurrently, the agent attempts to counteract this fall by moving the base abruptly to the right, applying a large rightward thrust after an initial small leftward action. This strong corrective motion is insufficient, however, as the pendulum continues its rapid divergence, resulting in a complete loss of control and an early termination of the episode.
