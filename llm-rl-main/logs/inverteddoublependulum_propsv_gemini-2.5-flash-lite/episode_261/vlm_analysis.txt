The agent begins with the inverted double pendulum almost perfectly upright and in a stable initial state. Over the course of 12 timesteps, the pendulum rapidly deviates from its vertical orientation, falling significantly to one side. Despite a very large corrective action being applied at the final timestep, the agent fails to re-stabilize the system. This results in an abrupt loss of control and the pendulum collapsing, leading to an early termination of the episode. The overall behavioral style is characterized by a rapid loss of control and a quick collapse.
