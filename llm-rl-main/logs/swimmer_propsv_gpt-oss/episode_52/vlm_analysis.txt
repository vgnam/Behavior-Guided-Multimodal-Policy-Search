The swimmer exhibits an oscillatory motion, characterized by repeated bending and straightening movements. Initially, it appears to be attempting to propel itself forward, but its motion is somewhat jerky and uncoordinated. The body bends sharply to one side and then the other, creating a wiggling effect rather than smooth forward progress. There are periods where the swimmer seems to gain some momentum, straightening out and moving forward, but these are often followed by sharp bends that disrupt the forward motion. This results in a somewhat erratic, back-and-forth movement with occasional forward surges. The overall behavioral style is best described as oscillatory control with periods of instability.
