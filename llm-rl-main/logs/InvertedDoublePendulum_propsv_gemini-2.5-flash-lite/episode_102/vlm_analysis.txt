The double pendulum initially attempts to stabilize by making small, rapid adjustments. The upper pendulum swings back and forth with increasing amplitude, while the base attempts to counteract these movements with sharp, alternating torques. This results in a jerky, oscillatory motion where the upper pendulum drifts further from the vertical. The movements become increasingly erratic and uncontrolled as the episode progresses. This behavior style can be described as chaotic motion with an initial attempt at oscillatory control that quickly deteriorates.
