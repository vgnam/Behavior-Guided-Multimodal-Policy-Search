The successful episodes also show a **consistent pattern of short forward bursts interleaved with brief pauses** that let the agent “sample” the corridor before committing to a turn, while the failures lack these pauses and instead execute **continuous aggressive actions (e.g., sustained action 2) that drive the state beyond safe limits**. Moreover, high‑reward trajectories tend to **delay sharp turns until a clear corner is reached**, using modest corrective actions (often action 0) only at well‑timed moments, whereas low‑reward runs apply sharp turns too early or too late, leading to collisions or drifts. This timing discipline prevents overshoot and keeps the agent inside the navigable corridor, directly translating into higher cumulative reward.
