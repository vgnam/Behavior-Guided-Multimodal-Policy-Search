This brief turn or brake curtails the forward thrust before the agent’s state variable can swing to extreme values, keeping the trajectory tighter and avoiding the large‑amplitude oscillations that dominate the low‑reward runs. By introducing the corrective action early, the high‑reward episodes also end sooner, limiting exposure to the stochastic disturbances that later cause runaway drift. In contrast, the low‑reward rollouts persist with unbroken forward motion, allowing the state to repeatedly overshoot and undershoot, which produces the severe collisions reflected in their more negative rewards.
