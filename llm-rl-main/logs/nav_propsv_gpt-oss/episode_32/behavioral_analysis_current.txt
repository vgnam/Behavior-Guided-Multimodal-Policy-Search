**4. TIMING** – The discriminative “smooth‑drift” and “no‑early‑spike” patterns manifest **very early**, typically within the first 10–15 steps of the episode. Because they occur at the start, they are likely causal: a stable early trajectory prevents the cascade of over‑corrections that later destroys performance. Consequently, the optimizer should prioritize parameters that govern the initial response to error (e.g., lower learning rate, stronger damping, smaller action‑output magnitude) to secure that early smooth behaviour.
