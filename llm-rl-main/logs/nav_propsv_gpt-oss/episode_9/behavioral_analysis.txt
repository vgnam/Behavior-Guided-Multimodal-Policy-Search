Pattern 2 (incremental turn corrections) appears **continuously from the early alignment phase onward**, acting as a stabilising feedback loop that prevents drift. In contrast, the failure‑inducing large thrust and abrupt turn happen **immediately at episode start**, suggesting they are the root cause of early terminations rather than downstream consequences.
