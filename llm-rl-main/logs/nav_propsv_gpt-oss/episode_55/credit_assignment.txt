[CURRENT POLICY]

[BEST POLICY]

The timing of the corrections in successful runs occurs within a single step after a forward move, preventing large jumps in the state variable, whereas failures exhibit sudden, multi‑step spikes in state before any correction is applied. Successful trajectories also stay within a moderate range of state values, using frequent small‑amplitude oscillations to “hug” the walls without colliding, while poor trajectories show rapid accelerations that push the agent into walls or out of the arena. Finally, the high‑reward behavior avoids sustained thrusts when near obstacles, opting instead for a turn‑then‑thrust pattern, whereas low‑reward rollouts persist with forward actions even when proximity cues suggest a need to steer, leading to crashes and large negative rewards.
