The high‑reward trajectories also limit back‑and‑forth oscillations, using only occasional, short‑duration adjustments, while the low‑reward cases exhibit repeated alternating left/right actions that amplify positional error and increase collision risk. Finally, the successful rollouts maintain a monotonic increase in state value throughout most of the episode, reflecting consistent progress toward the goal, whereas the failing rollouts show abrupt drops in state right before termination, indicating loss of control that directly depresses the cumulative reward.
