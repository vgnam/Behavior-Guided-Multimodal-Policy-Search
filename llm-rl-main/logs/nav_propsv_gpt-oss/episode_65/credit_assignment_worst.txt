This timing contrast means the high‑reward trajectories maintain momentum and avoid excessive state fluctuations, reducing time spent in inefficient back‑and‑forth motions that accrue negative reward. Conversely, the low‑reward behavior’s continual over‑correction and internal‑state spikes waste steps and increase the chance of collisions, producing the much larger penalty.
