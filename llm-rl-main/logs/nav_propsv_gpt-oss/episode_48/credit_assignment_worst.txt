The timing difference—early, purposeful deviation versus prolonged, unchecked forward motion—means the high‑reward episodes incur fewer per‑step penalties and avoid the late‑stage crash that drags down the low‑reward scores. Consequently, the presence of an early, varied action and a brief, directed maneuver is the key behavioral component that consistently raises the reward in the better worst‑policy rollouts.
